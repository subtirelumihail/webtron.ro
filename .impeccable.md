## Design Context

### Users

Visitors landing on webtron.ro—often checking who is behind a project, a referral link, or a business card. They need a fast read: what this is, how to reach out, where it is based. Attention is short; the page should feel calm and confident, not sales-heavy.

### Brand Personality

Precise · spare · human-made (three words)

Tone: solo operator, direct sentences, no corporate plural. The site is a quiet signpost, not a brochure.

### Aesthetic Direction

Brutally minimal editorial: plenty of whitespace, one strong typographic moment, a single warm accent on tinted neutrals. Light mode first (daytime / professional context). Anti-reference: gradient hero blobs, icon grids, “AI slop” cyan-purple-dark, card stacks, team photo walls.

### Design Principles

1. **One job**: make contact obvious within seconds.
2. **Nothing decorative without purpose**—motion explains hierarchy and arrival, not decoration.
3. **First-person or neutral copy only**—never imply a larger organization than exists.
4. **OKLCH + tinted neutrals**—no pure black/white; accent used rarely.
5. **Accessible by default**: focus states, contrast, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
